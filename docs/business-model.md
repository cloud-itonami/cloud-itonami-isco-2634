- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-2634`

## Customer

- individual clients
- schools
- employee-assistance programs

## Offer

- intake and assessment
- session scheduling
- documentation support
- crisis-escalation pathway
- reporting

## Revenue

- per-session fee
- monthly practice-management platform fee

## Trust Controls

- no crisis-risk assessment automation without governor gate
- crisis disclosures always escalate to human clinician
- session records are auditable, not editable
